### Abel W. Sugebo

Information-integrity and OSINT researcher in Washington, DC. I investigate influence operations, coordinated manipulation, and platform abuse, with a focus on Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa and a growing focus on AI-enabled threats.

From 2021 to 2025 I led investigations at Inform Africa and its fact-checking arm HaqCheck (Ethiopia's first IFCN Verified Signatory), and served as Inform Africa's principal contact in Meta's Trusted Partner program for content, account, and imminent-harm escalations. Certified DISARM analyst. Native Amharic speaker.

**What I use this account for:** reproducible research workflows (collection scripts, codebooks, evidence ledgers, and tests) that make my investigative methods inspectable.
